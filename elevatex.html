SOCIAL IMPACT · LEADERSHIP · ENTREPRENEURSHIP

ElevateX

A student-led nonprofit in India focused on financial literacy, leadership and real-world impact, built so that student initiatives become a lasting organisation rather than one-off events.

Founded by Vivaan Agarwal · 2025

ORIGIN

From an idea during board exams to a student-led organization.

ElevateX was first conceptualized on 8 March 2025. What began as an idea to create meaningful student-led impact evolved into a structured organization built around leadership, ownership and execution.

The underlying problem was straightforward: many student initiatives have strong intent but struggle with continuity. Activities happen, but the systems needed to sustain them often do not.

ElevateX was built around a different approach: create an organization where students could take ownership, operate within defined structures and execute initiatives that could continue beyond individual events.

THE PROBLEM

Good intentions do not automatically create sustained impact.

Many youth-led initiatives operate as isolated efforts. A drive happens, an event is completed and the activity ends.

The deeper gap is organizational: without defined roles, processes and accountability, it becomes difficult to maintain momentum or scale beyond a small group.

ElevateX was designed to address that gap by treating student impact as an organizational and leadership problem, not simply an events problem.

ORGANIZATIONAL MODEL

A platform rather than a collection of events.

ElevateX operates through decentralized teams with centralized coordination. Students are given defined responsibilities and ownership over initiatives while remaining part of a larger organizational structure.

PROGRAMS

Three areas of execution.

Project Unnati

A financial literacy initiative delivering workshops on investing, budgeting and real-world economics through collaborations with schools and student communities.

Welfare Drives

Community initiatives including food distribution, environmental clean-ups, plantation drives and grassroots impact projects.

Fundraising

Large-scale campaigns designed to mobilize students and raise funds for social-impact initiatives.

FLAGSHIP INITIATIVE

Euphoria

Euphoria became ElevateX's largest and most ambitious fundraiser, bringing together event planning, fundraising, sponsorships, operations and student mobilization.

The event attracted more than 500 attendees and generated over ₹6 lakh in revenue. After accounting for event expenses, Euphoria generated approximately ₹90,000 in profit for ElevateX's social-impact work.

LEADERSHIP

Building systems around ownership.

I founded ElevateX with Samridh Sanganeria and Devyansh Tolasaria, initially taking responsibility for building the organization's structure and direction.

As Founder, Former CEO and Chairman, my focus was on creating systems that allowed other students to take ownership rather than making the organization dependent on its founders.

That meant defining roles, building departments, establishing processes and creating a culture in which people felt responsible for what they were building.

WHAT I LEARNED

People stay when they feel ownership.

Building ElevateX changed how I think about leadership. Structure matters, but structure alone does not create a lasting organization.

The real challenge is creating an environment in which people feel that the organization belongs to them. Ownership creates responsibility, and responsibility creates continuity.

That became one of the central lessons I took away from building ElevateX.
