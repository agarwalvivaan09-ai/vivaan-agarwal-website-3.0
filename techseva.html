TechSeva

A grassroots e-waste collection initiative focused on responsible disposal, recycling and environmental awareness.

Founded and led by Vivaan Agarwal

THE ORIGIN

The idea started with a drawer.

The idea for TechSeva didn't start as a project. It started with a drawer.

While cleaning my room, I opened a drawer filled with old cables, broken devices and unused electronics that had been sitting there for years.

It felt insignificant at first. Just clutter.

When I asked if I could throw everything out, I was told that e-waste can be extremely harmful if it is not disposed of properly. That moment shifted my perspective.

If I had this much e-waste sitting unused, it was likely that many others did too.

What looked like a small personal problem was actually part of a much larger one.

That realization led to TechSeva: an initiative focused on collecting e-waste from local households and ensuring it was responsibly recycled.

THE PROBLEM

Electronic waste is easy to ignore.

E-waste is a growing environmental challenge, yet awareness around proper disposal remains limited at the household level.

Old cables, broken electronics and unused devices are often stored indefinitely or discarded without knowing whether they will eventually be handled responsibly.

TechSeva focused on addressing this problem at the level where it begins: individual households and local communities.

THE APPROACH

Turning household clutter into a collection system.

TechSeva was initiated as a focused effort to collect e-waste directly from local households, beginning with family, friends and immediate community networks.

The initiative combined outreach, awareness and logistics with coordination with a recycling organization to ensure responsible disposal.

KEY INSIGHT

Environmental impact does not always begin at scale.

TechSeva reinforced a simple lesson for me: meaningful impact can begin with identifying a problem that exists in your own surroundings.

The important part is not simply recognizing the problem. It is building a process that turns awareness into action and action into measurable outcomes.
