CONSUMER BRAND · ENTREPRENEURSHIP · PRODUCT

MIST

A student-led consumer venture built around a simple idea: make fragrance easier to carry.

Founded by Vivaan Agarwal

THE PROBLEM

Fragrance is useful. Carrying a full bottle isn't.

A few hours after applying perfume in the morning, the fragrance can fade. Carrying a full-sized bottle throughout the day, however, is inconvenient.

Portable perfume atomizers offered a simple solution, but many options available to younger consumers were either poorly positioned or unnecessarily expensive.

That raised a straightforward question:

Why wasn't there an affordable, compact option designed around everyday student life?

ORIGIN

A small everyday problem became a consumer venture.

MIST started on a hot summer afternoon in school.

After applying perfume in the morning, the fragrance had faded by later in the day. Carrying the original bottle everywhere was inconvenient.

I began looking into portable alternatives and researching suppliers and wholesalers.

That research led to compact refillable perfume atomizers and eventually to the idea of building a consumer brand around them.

What began as a solution to a small everyday problem evolved into a functioning venture involving product sourcing, branding, sales, team management and investor engagement.

THE PRODUCT

A smaller way to carry the fragrance you already use.

MIST is a slim refillable fragrance atomizer designed for people who already have a fragrance they love and want a smaller, more convenient way to carry it.

01

Compact Format

Designed for pockets, school bags, gym kits, handbags and travel pouches.

02

Metal Exterior

A metal-look exterior provides a more solid, durable feel while protecting the fragrance during everyday carry.

03

Fill Window

A clear fill window makes it possible to see the amount of fragrance inside the atomizer.

04

Bottom-Valve Refill

The bottom-valve system allows the atomizer to be refilled directly from compatible perfume spray stems.

HOW IT WORKS

Refill in three steps.

01

Remove the perfume cap

Take off the spray head from the perfume bottle to expose the refill stem.

02

Press MIST onto the stem

Align the bottom valve and press down steadily until the chamber fills.

03

Carry it with confidence

Keep MIST with you throughout the day without carrying the original perfume bottle.

BUILDING THE BUSINESS

The product was only the starting point.

Building MIST meant moving beyond the product itself. I worked across sourcing, packaging, branding, pricing, customer acquisition and sales.

The venture used a direct-to-consumer model alongside exhibition and event-based sales, allowing us to observe customer reactions directly and iterate on positioning and pricing.

Product Sourcing

Identifying suppliers and evaluating product suitability.

Brand Development

Building the MIST identity and positioning the product for younger consumers.

Sales

Combining direct sales with exhibition and event-based distribution.

Customer Feedback

Using direct customer interaction to understand demand, pricing and product-market fit.

JOURNEY

Iterating through the market.

MIST evolved through multiple iterations of branding, pricing, packaging and customer acquisition.

Each sale created another data point about consumer behavior: what people valued, what price points felt reasonable, how they responded to the product and what influenced their purchase decisions.

That made MIST as much a learning exercise in entrepreneurship as it was a product venture.
