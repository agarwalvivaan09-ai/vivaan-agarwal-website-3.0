Featured research

Household Financial Resilience Index (HFRI)

Independent research · Quantitative finance · 2026

A transparent mathematical index that turns savings, debt, emergency liquidity and spending stability into a single 0–100 score for how much of a financial shock a household can absorb. Built with calibrated scoring functions, a 1,000-household simulation, weight-sensitivity testing and an ONS-style benchmark. It has been implemented into Arbor.

4dimensions: savings, debt, emergency cover, expense stability
1,000simulated households, 12 months each
0.97+rank correlation across 10–40% weight tests
0–100open, fully published scoring model

India Pays China $99 Billion to Compete with China

Research Fellow · Nikore Associates · Young Economist Programme · 2026

Research on India's industrial policy, Production Linked Incentive schemes and the country's $99.2 billion trade deficit with China.

I examined India's dependence on Chinese components, connected trade data with domestic manufacturing capacity, and developed policy recommendations aimed at strengthening domestic production and reducing supply-chain dependence.

Purulia · January 2026
