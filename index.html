I’m a Class XII student at La Martiniere for Boys, Kolkata, working at the intersection of economics, finance, quantitative research and entrepreneurship.

My work ranges from India’s industrial policy and trade dependence to the Household Financial Resilience Index, a quantitative framework now implemented in Arbor, and from running a student-led nonprofit to working on real manufacturing problems.

Most of what I learn comes from building, trying, failing, and figuring things out along the way.
