Featured research · Quantitative finance · 2026

Household Financial Resilience Index

A transparent mathematical index that turns savings, debt, emergency liquidity and spending stability into a single 0–100 score for how much of a financial shock a household can absorb. Built with calibrated scoring functions, a 1,000-household simulation, weight-sensitivity testing and an ONS-style benchmark. It has been implemented into Arbor.
